CHAPTER 02 • CAMERA PROTOCOLS

Camera Acquisition Subsystem

Comprehensive documentation on configuring industrial optical sensors, USB3 devices, network IP streams, synthetic part simulation, and network auto-discovery.

01

Supported Acquisition Protocols

Protocol Syntax / Address Description
CameraProtocol.USB 0, 1, 2 DirectShow / V4L2 USB camera index for webcams and USB3 industrial sensors.
CameraProtocol.RTSP rtsp://user:pass@192.168.1.100:554/live H.264 / H.265 RTSP network video stream from IP cameras and industrial surveillance.
CameraProtocol.HTTP http://192.168.1.100/snapshot.jpg High-resolution single-frame snapshot capture over HTTP/HTTPS.
CameraProtocol.TEST_STREAM Synthetic Part Simulator Generates moving electronic PCBs with IC components, pads, drilled holes, and surface scratches.

Synthetic Industrial Part Generator

For offline application development, recipe training, or software validation without physical camera hardware, select CameraProtocol.TEST_STREAM. The generator synthesizes an industrial inspection line featuring:

  • A printed circuit board (PCB) traveling along an automated conveyor at consistent velocity.
  • An integrated circuit (IC) package undergoing realistic angular rotations and position offsets.
  • Fiducial alignment crosses and drilled mounting holes with randomized subpixel diameters.
  • Simulated surface scratch anomalies for flaw detection verification.

Network Auto-Discovery Daemon (Port 1069)

VisionStudio runs a background UDP responder on port 1069. Industrial setup tools can locate VisionStudio instances on the local network subnet, retrieving device hostnames, IP addresses, MAC addresses, and active inspection status.

02

Clean Standby & Dynamic Connect/Disconnect

Clean Standby Operational State

To guarantee deterministic workstation startup and prevent background process locks on USB video devices, VisionStudio initiates in a protected Clean Standby state:

  • No Background Hardware Locking: Physical USB and RTSP handles remain closed upon software launch, allowing other applications or configuration tools to access sensor registers.
  • Dynamic Connect Button: Located in the main toolbar, styled in industrial red (#E74C3C) when disconnected, indicating "Connect Camera". Clicking this button smoothly spins up background acquisition threads and transitions to bright green (#2ECC71) indicating "Disconnect Camera".
  • Standby Reticle & Grid: While offline, the high-performance OpenGL/Qt viewport renders an animated dark grid, crosshair calibration target, and status telemetry callouts.
03

Camera Optical Metrology & Calibration Dialog

In precision manufacturing and robot guidance, measuring raw pixel values is insufficient. VisionStudio includes a built-in Interactive Visual Camera Calibration & Metrology Dialog accessible from the main toolbar button 📐 Calibration.

Interactive Visual Camera Calibration & Scale Factor
📏

Live On-Screen Ruler Drawing

Click & drag anywhere on the viewfinder canvas to draw a brand-new measurement line. Interactive dual endpoint handles (Cyan P1 and Emerald Green P2) and perpendicular caliper end jaws allow precision reticle placement directly over physical scale ticks.

❄️

Freeze Frame & Subpixel Zoom

Click ❄ Freeze Frame to freeze camera motion during conveyor vibration, allowing operators to zoom in (🔍+ or mouse wheel) and pan to place reticles with subpixel accuracy before resuming live stream.

⚡

Quick Presets & Live Calculation

Select instant physical dimension presets (10 mm, 50 mm, 100 mm, 150 mm, 300 mm) or enter custom gauge values. The dialog dynamically updates the linear scale ratio (mm/px) and optical resolution (px/mm).

🎯

Automatic Recipe Propagation

When saving calibration, the scale factor and engineering unit are automatically pushed to all active Industrial Caliper & Measurement steps in your recipe, instantly converting edge and contour measurements from pixels to millimeters.

Step-by-Step Floor Scale Calibration Procedure

  1. Position Calibration Reference: Place a certified 100 mm ruler, scale gauge, or step block on the floor or conveyor surface directly within the camera field of view.
  2. Open Calibration Dialog: Click 📐 Calibration in the main toolbar. The live camera stream will open in the viewfinder.
  3. Freeze Video (Optional): If lighting flickers or parts move, click ❄ Freeze Frame to capture a still frame for calm adjustment.
  4. Draw On-Screen Ruler: Left-click at the 0 mm mark on the ruler image and drag across to the 100 mm mark. Two precision circular reticles will appear (Cyan P1 at 0 mm, Emerald Green P2 at 100 mm).
  5. Fine-Tune Endpoints: Use the mouse wheel to zoom in on the ruler tick marks. Drag P1 or P2 individually to snap reticles exactly onto the index edges. Drag the center of the line to translate without changing length.
  6. Enter Physical Dimension: In the control drawer, click the 100 mm preset button (or type 100.0 in the Known Physical Size spinbox).
  7. Apply Calibration: Verify the computed ratio (e.g., 1 px = 0.2000 mm; 5.00 px / mm). Keep "Update all Caliper / Measurement steps in recipe" checked and click ✓ Apply & Save Calibration. All calipers immediately display true calibrated metric dimensions.
